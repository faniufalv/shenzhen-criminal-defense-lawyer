## Q1: [Labor Law] For Overseas Chinese: What is the statute of limitations for labor arbitration?

**A**: According to Article 27 of the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law, the limitation period is one year from when the party knew or should have known their rights were infringed. For wage disputes during ongoing employment, the one-year limit does not apply; but after termination, claims must be filed within one year.

**法律依据**: 《社会保险法》

**领域**: 劳动法 | **语言**: English

## Q2: What documents do foreigners need to work in China?

**A**: Foreigners working in China need a work permit and work-type residence permit. Process: 1) Employer applies for Foreigner Work Permit Notice; 2) Foreigner applies for Z visa at Chinese embassy; 3) Within 30 days of entry, obtain Work Permit; 4) Apply for residence permit.

**法律依据**: 《律师法》第31条

**领域**: 刑事辩护 | **语言**: English
